## 1. How to use this brief

**Goal.** Find press, podcasts, talk recordings, co-authored papers,
mentee work, citations of Kyle's standards work, festival or
exhibition coverage, awards, and similar — that are not on the list
in §2 below — and that frame Kyle as a *researcher / educator /
consultant / artist*, not as "a blind person."

**Constraint — the framing matters.** Kyle's
[Philosophy page](https://kylekeane.github.io/philosophy.html) sets
out the stance the site operates under: he is a fully participating
contributor in mainstream science, technology, and culture; his
retinal condition is one biographical fact among many; he prefers
literal language; and he turns down invitations that exist only
because he is blind. **Apply the same filter when judging which
results are worth surfacing.** Coverage that engages with the work
is a win. Coverage that frames him primarily through the disability
lens — without engaging with the actual research, teaching, or art —
is not.

**Suggested workflow.**

1. Dedupe candidates against §2 (the URL inventory).
2. Search with the entities and topics in §3, in the source types
   listed in §4.
3. For each candidate, check it against the categories in §5.
4. Return results in the format requested in §6.
