## 4. “ECHO takeover” at Rockefeller Center — with Joshue Ott / SuperDraw

This is by far the best-documented installation. Primary sources captured:

### 4a. Echo installation — verified facts (primary source: resound-nyc.com)

- **Title:** *ECHO* (part of the “BOUNDLESS” sequence inside *reSOUND New York*).
- **Venue:** HERO at Rockefeller Center, 610 5th Avenue, Rink Level, New York, NY 10020  (https://resound-nyc.com/).
- **Run:** 1 October 2025 – 31 October 2026, daily 11am–7pm (last entry 6pm)  (https://resound-nyc.com/).
- **Presenting / commissioning entities:** d’strict (Korean immersive-art studio) and MATTE Projects (HERO operator).  Editorial dated **20 November 2025** (https://matteprojects.com/editorial/resound-new-york-hero).
- **Original debut:** Seoul (Culture Station Seoul 284) before NYC; reSOUND drew over 110,000 visitors in Seoul. 
- **Echo created in 2024** as part of “the first series of d’strict Art Project”   (https://resound-nyc.com/artwork/echo).
- **Named ECHO collaborators (per resound-nyc.com/artwork/echo):**
  - **d’strict Art Project** (creative direction)
  - **Dr. Erin Kara** — Professor, MIT Department of Physics; observational astrophysicist   (provided black-hole observation data)
  - **Ian Condry** — founder of the MIT Spatial Sound Lab  
  - **Kyle Keane** — “Professor at University of Bristol, UK, who developed sonification from black hole data”  
  - **oOps.50656** — “a new media composer and artist collective that produced eight-channel sonification and sound design for the project”  
  - **KKOL Studio** — “created the light and sound installation”  
- **Technical / curatorial framing (verbatim from resound-nyc.com/artwork/echo):**

> “*ECHO* features ‘Kinetic Sound,’ a dynamic integration of a soundscape derived from black hole observation data and light installation. Light, cast by a centrally placed moving sodium lamp and sound, played through an eight-channel system, recreate the movement of energy around a black hole… The work spatially embodies the immense gravitational distortion of a black hole, as well as physical theories including the Doppler effect and general relativity, which are translated into a sensory experience from the perspective of photons, the smallest particles of light.”  
- **Medium:** “Installation, 8-channel kinetic sound and light, 2024.”  
- **Black-hole data being sonified:** Per the underlying MIT/Kara collaboration, the sonification represents X-ray “light echoes” off the accretion disks of stellar-mass black holes (the V404 Cygni system and similar), with frequency mapped to pitch (lower-frequency light = lower-pitch sound).  Animation by Michal Dovciak (ASU CAS);  sound computed by Kyle Keane and Erin Kara  (https://news.mit.edu/2022/search-reveals-eight-new-sources-black-hole-echoes-0502; https://quest.mit.edu/news/intersensory-data-representations-black-hole). The published peer-reviewed work that drove the sonification is Kara et al. (2022) on eight new black-hole-echo sources.
- **Quotes from Keane on the resound-nyc.com home page (verbatim):**

> “One of the most fantastic and important things about art is that it gives us a chance to investigate through different systems of exploration.” 
> “It’s been a wonderful experience, and I really hope the audience members take a moment to appreciate what an absolutely amazing phenomenon it is that we can perceive the world around us.”  — Kyle Keane  (https://resound-nyc.com/)

### 4b. Joshue Ott / SuperDraw — verified background

- Joshue Ott (b. 1977, Brooklyn) is the creator of **superDraw**, a software instrument for hand-drawn forms manipulated in real time and projected at large scale (https://www.joshueott.com/, https://www.joshueott.com/about). He runs Interval Studios  and has been resident at Eyebeam and Times Square Arts; 2022 NYFA Fellowship in Architecture and Environmental Design;  performances at Mutek, Carnegie Hall, Ars Electronica, Baryshnikov Arts Center,  Le Poisson Rouge, Roulette, and others.
- Independently of the takeover, Ott was also confirmed as a live performer at **Dissolve Music 2024 at MIT** on 3 October 2024 (Thursday): “8:20pm Joshue Ott (live) + talkback”  (https://spatialsoundlab.mit.edu/dissolve-music-2024/) — establishing that Ott has a working relationship with the same Spatial Sound Lab cohort around Keane.

### 4c. The “ECHO takeover” event with Ott / Keane — could not verify

- I could not find any public web documentation, press, or social-media post (other than Kyle’s potential LinkedIn, which I could not access) describing a takeover event in which Kyle joined Joshue Ott inside the ECHO installation with a handheld speaker and a SuperDraw render specialized for the room. Searches against resound-nyc.com programs page, MATTE editorial, joshueott.com, Time Out, and general press yielded nothing.
- The reSOUND program page lists past “Immersive Performance” / “Listening Experience” events — Rosalia LUX, Joy Lee (31 Jan 2026), Haze Kim (20 Dec 2025), Vivake Khamsingsavath (6 Dec 2025), Hannah Bigeleisen workshop (18 Dec 2025)  (https://resound-nyc.com/) — but **no Keane/Ott listing surfaced**.
- It is plausible the takeover was a private/unticketed activation not added to the public program page; documentation may exist only on Kyle’s, Joshue’s, or MATTE/d’strict’s internal channels (and on the user’s own LinkedIn).

**Recommendation:** Write the ECHO installation portion with confidence using the resound-nyc.com text. For the takeover specifically, the artist statement should rely on Kyle’s first-hand account, naming Joshue Ott + SuperDraw with the citations above, but should not assert a date or press coverage.
