About Me
Replace text with:
Dr. Kyle Keane is a Senior Lecturer in Assistive Technologies at the University of Bristol, where he contributes to innovative research and teaching at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human cognition, and inclusive technology design. His work focuses on building intelligent, perception-aware systems that expand how people, particularly those with disabilities, interact with complex information through multisensory experiences.
Kyle’s motivation is deeply personal. Born with a degenerative retinal condition, his lived experience with progressively changing vision has provided unique insight and empathy that drive his passion for designing technologies that are not only accessible, but also transformative. He brings a multidisciplinary approach to his work, drawing on a background in quantum computing and computational physics to bring scientific rigor, design precision, and human-centered values to his work.
Prior to joining the University of Bristol, Kyle spent over a decade at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), where he held roles as a research scientist, lecturer, and education lead in experiential learning initiatives. His contributions spanned machine learning, accessible computing, and the development of open-source tools that make STEM fields more inclusive for blind and visually impaired learners.
Kyle’s current research explores intersensory perception, how information can be represented and understood across auditory, tactile, and other non-visual modalities. He is pioneering new ways to sonify and physicalize data, creating tools that make computational science and artificial intelligence more accessible and intuitive for all users.
Throughout his career, Kyle has worked in diverse roles across academia, startups, and industry, including as a physicist, software engineer, UX designer, instructional strategist, and research director. His work lives at the intersection of design, engineering, education, assistive technology, and disability advocacy.
Beyond research, Kyle is deeply committed to wellness, community building, and reframing the narrative around disability, not as a deficit but as a source of creativity, resilience, and insight into our shared human experience.

I approach creativity as both an artistic practice and a scientific exploration. My creative work is rooted in deep curiosity and a commitment to accessibility. I design experiences that translate the invisible into the tangible: sonifying the warping of spacetime, crafting AI-powered tools for non-visual cognition, and building tactile-auditory landscapes that help people understand complex ideas through embodied interaction. Whether in research labs, hacker spaces, or co-design workshops, I thrive on the creative collision of disciplines and perspectives.
From MIT to Bristol, and from international collaborations to local communities, I am intrigued to explore opportunities at the intersection of science, art, and inclusion. I believe some of the most impactful technologies emerge from asking wild questions, following intuition, and building strange, beautiful things with others. If you're curious about quantum consciousness, perception-hacking AI, or storytelling across sensory dimensions, I’m always open to a good conversation and the chance to co-create something radical.
