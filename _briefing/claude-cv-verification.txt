## Complete publication record and scholarly impact

### Quantum computing and superconducting qubits (PhD era: 2007-2012)

1. **Kyle Keane and Alexander N. Korotkov**, “Simplified quantum error detection and correction for superconducting qubits,” Physical Review A 86, 012333 (2012) 
- Novel approaches for protecting quantum information using simple protocols 
- Demonstrated that for qubits with energy relaxation, repetitive N-qubit codes can be used for quantum error detection but not correction 
- Proposed two-qubit algorithms readily realizable with phase qubit technology 
1. **Alexander N. Korotkov and Kyle Keane**, “Decoherence suppression by quantum measurement reversal,” Physical Review A 81, 040103(R) (2010) - Rapid Communication 
- Proposed novel protocol utilizing weak quantum measurements to protect quantum information  
- First procedure to protect a single qubit from energy relaxation using weak measurement reversal without additional qubits and entanglement  
1. **Kyle Keane**, “Quantum State Protection and Transfer Using Superconducting Qubits,” PhD Dissertation, University of California, Riverside (2012)
