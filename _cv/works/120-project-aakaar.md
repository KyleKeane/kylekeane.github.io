An international 3D-printing exchange that grew out of the Humanistic
Co-Design Initiative's January 2019 LVPEI workshop: founded by three
local students (Shantanu Landore, Sarthak Kapoor, and Daksh Pamar),
supervised by me as a volunteer through the EPICS programme at NIT
Warangal with programme manager Radhika Manoharan, and deployed at
Smt. Kamalabai Educational Institution in Bangalore through Prithvi
Raj and Srinivas Kuppa — middle-school students in Cambridge,
Massachusetts (Marnie Klein's class at Buckingham Browne & Nichols
School) and India co-designing and 3D-printing tactile teaching aids
for blind students. Ranked a top-five national Social Impact project
by Education World Magazine (No. 2 in Bangalore); extended to Gujarat
via Ankit Budhia with support from Dr. Satish Tripathi of Ek Kadam
Aur; analysed in the MIT Sloan SDM master's thesis of Indrayud Mandal
([*Assessing the feasibility of a circular economy for 3D printed
tactile educational aids for visually impaired (VI) students in
India*, 2021, which I supervised](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/140078));
selected as a
top-twenty team with seed funding in
[the 2021 MIT PKG IDEAS Social Innovation Challenge](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mit-ideas-social-innovation-challenge-tara-cunningham).
Documented in
[the MIT MISTI retrospective](https://misti.mit.edu/humanistic-design-workshops-india-impact-and-reach).
