An international 3D-printing exchange that grew out of the Humanistic
Co-Design Initiative's January 2019 LVPEI workshop: middle-school
students in Cambridge, Massachusetts and India co-designing and
3D-printing tactile teaching aids for blind students. Documented in
[the MIT MISTI retrospective](https://misti.mit.edu/humanistic-design-workshops-india-impact-and-reach);
top-20 in the MIT PKG IDEAS Social Innovation Challenge 2021.
