The global incubator, accelerator, and mentorship programme I founded
at MIT: a cooperative of individuals, organisations, and institutions
developing assistive technology with — not for — people with
disabilities, through year-long co-design fellowships seeded by
intensive launch weekends. Programmes ran across India (Delhi,
Chennai, Hyderabad), Saudi Arabia (the CoCreate fellowship), and
Uruguay, with spin-offs including Project Aakaar. The founding story
and partner network are on
the [Teaching](/teaching.html#international-workshops-and-intensives) page.
