A year-long collaboration with Prof. Erin Kara (MIT Physics) and Ian
Condry (MIT Spatial Sound Lab) translating X-ray light-echo data from
stellar-mass black-hole binaries into audible sound — hundreds of
iterations to make the physics perceivable by ear. The sonification
reached the *New York Times*, CNN, and syndication in at least five
languages, appears on the *Black Hole Reverb* album, and became the
heart of the *ECHO* installation. Listen on
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iIeIag2Ji8k); the full story
is on [Every sense at full resolution](/every-sense.html).
