I marched in the Ruben S. Ayala High School indoor percussion
ensemble in the era of its first World Championship medals — the
[1999 WGI Percussion Scholastic A World Championship](https://www.wgi.org/historical_score_per/1999/)
and the
[2002 Scholastic Open silver medal](https://www.wgi.org/historical_score_per/2002/)
— the foundation of what became the most decorated scholastic
percussion programme in WGI history. The ensemble training in
technique, listening, and group co-ordination runs directly into my
spatial-sound practice today.
