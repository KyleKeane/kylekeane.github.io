A one-day symposium I hosted at The Watershed, Bristol (14 July 2025)
on innovation at the meeting point of assistive technology, art, and
echolocation — keynote by Daniel Kish (World Access for the Blind),
with speakers from the RCA and Concordia and student research
demonstrations.
[Event page](https://biglab.co.uk/event/emergent-harmonics/) and
[session recordings on YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLuDhYTU_hDHWav0RJb4qKESUsSpiYFg_M).
