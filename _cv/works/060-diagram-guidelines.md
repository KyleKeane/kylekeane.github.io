The 2014 DIAGRAM Center report *Interactive Scientific Graphics:
Recommended Practices for Verbal Description* (with Christina
Laverentz): guidelines for making interactive scientific content
perceivable in words. The recommendation to separate intent from
action informed the creation of the W3C User-Intent Working Group;
the guidelines were adopted by PhET Interactive Simulations and are
cited in the PhET team's accessibility research. This standards work
underpins my current audio-tactile graphics research.
