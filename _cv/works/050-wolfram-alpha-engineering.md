At Wolfram Research I designed and shipped the first software system
for automated step-by-step solution of calculations including physical
units — deployed to millions of students through the
[Wolfram|Alpha step-by-step solver](https://www.wolframalpha.com/examples/pro-features/step-by-step-solutions)
— and wrote the XML parser and natural-language generation that let
Apple's Siri speak quantitative results from Wolfram|Alpha: the first
voice-assistant integration that spoke computational answers.
