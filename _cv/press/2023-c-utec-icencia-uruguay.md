[Casi 500 jóvenes aprendieron a programar y generar soluciones en equipo — UTEC (Spanish)](https://utec.edu.uy/es/noticia/casi-500-jovenes-aprendieron-a-programar-y-generar-soluciones-en-equipo-a-traves-de-un-proyecto-de-utec-innova-y-la-embajada-de-ee-uu/),
January 2023
([English version](https://utec.edu.uy/en/article/casi-500-jovenes-aprendieron-a-programar-y-generar-soluciones-en-equipo-a-traves-de-un-proyecto-de-utec-innova-y-la-embajada-de-ee-uu/)).
UTEC's retrospective on the iCiencia programme — a US Embassy of
Uruguay Small Grant with support credited to MIT — naming me as
co-developer, with UTEC docente Jorge Gutierrez, of the *Arduino for
creative collaboration* workshop: 18 workshops across five cities in
2021–2022, nearly 500 participants, 90+ hours of training across 18
institutions, with inclusive-design examples including a call device
for a person with a disability.
