## Roles

### Senior Lecturer in Assistive Technologies

University of Bristol — School of Computer Science, June 2024 – present.
Teaching and research focusing on multisensory interaction, human–computer
interaction, immersive audio, agentic AI, human echolocation and
localisation, fingertip haptics, and perception-aware robotics. Continuing
affiliation as Researcher at the
[MIT Spatial Sound Lab](https://spatialsoundlab.mit.edu/), co-leading the
*Accessible Technology and Disability Justice* research theme with
Nelly Kate Anderson.

### Lecturer

Massachusetts Institute of Technology — Electrical Engineering and Computer
Science, September 2022 – May 2024. Lead faculty for *Principles and
Practices of Assistive Technology* (cross-listed as 6.450 / 6.811 / 2.78 /
HST.420), the capstone engineering subject pairing student teams with a
community member to co-invent technology that improves their independence
on a specific task. Co-instructor of *6.3900: Introduction to Machine
Learning*.

### AI Research Scientist

MIT — College of Computing, Quest for Intelligence, September 2019 –
September 2022. AI research consultant and researcher across multiple labs,
building capacity for interdisciplinary research across human learning,
brain and cognitive science, AI, and societal modelling. Projects:
automated metadata tagging of unstructured humanities and social-science
datasets; AI vs human performance on out-of-distribution facial
recognition; AI-enhanced tactification of computer graphics; an
open-source visual-stimulus repository for human-vs-machine vision
comparison; statistical forecasting of infectious-disease spread in urban
universities.

### Lecturer (Materials Science)

MIT — Materials Science and Engineering, June 2015 – September 2019.
Created and led *3.008: Humanistic Co-design of Assistive Technology in the
Developing World*, a three-week subject taking students to India to work
with local people with disabilities. Co-taught undergraduate subjects
including *Computational Methods for Materials Science and Engineering*,
*Principles and Practices of Assistive Technology*, and *Making for
Innovation, Science, and Outreach*. Computational Curriculum Advisor on the
Undergraduate Committee. Staff sponsor and advisor for MIT's Assistive
Technology Hackathon (ATHack).

### Research Scientist (Interactive Materials Education Laboratory)

MIT — Materials Science and Engineering, June 2015 – September 2019.
Founder and director of an undergraduate research lab using technology to
increase public engagement, data literacy, and science education through
designed interactive experiences.

### Technical Consultant

Wolfram Research — Communications and Strategy, October 2015 – September
2019. Educational outreach programmes including hackathon sponsorships and
mentorship; accessibility-related technical advisement and disability
advocacy training; rapid prototyping of new technologies and applications.

### Research Scientist (Solid-State Dewetting)

MIT — Carl V. Thompson Research Group, Materials Science and Engineering,
January 2016 – January 2017. Led the development of a large-scale
atomistic kinetic Monte Carlo simulation for solid-state dewetting in thin
metal films, GPU-parallelised using Bayesian statistics.

### Head of User Experience

Indico Data Solutions, March 2015 – October 2015. Managed website design
and usability, community engagement, developer relations, and the
documentation of a RESTful machine-learning API.

### Research Programmer — Educational Software

Wolfram Research — Educational Technology and Software, January 2014 –
March 2015. Designed and shipped the first software system for automated
step-by-step solution of calculations including physical units —
deployed to millions of students through the
[Wolfram\|Alpha step-by-step solver](https://www.wolframalpha.com/examples/pro-features/step-by-step-solutions).
Built a customisable curriculum-development platform for educators
teaching computer programming to children.

### Research Programmer — Special Projects

Wolfram Research — Special Projects, November 2012 – December 2013.
Designed and programmed accessibility features for blind users of
Wolfram\|Alpha, CDF Player, and Mathematica. Wrote grants for
disability-access research, including qualitative and quantitative
data-collection design.

### R&D Fellow

Wolfram Research — Special Projects, July 2012 – October 2012.
Comprehensive review of the company's products for usability by people
with disabilities. Prepared Section 508 Voluntary Product Accessibility
Templates for all major products. Created a functioning workaround for
completely blind users to access Mathematica's computational capabilities.

### Graduate Student Researcher

University of California, Riverside — Department of Electrical
Engineering, with Dr. Alexander Korotkov, January 2009 – August 2012.
Weak quantum measurements, uncollapsing, quantum error correction and
detection, decoherence suppression, and quantum state and process
tomography for quantum computation and communication. Procedures
proposed in this work were subsequently verified in the originally
proposed superconducting-qubit system and others.

### Teaching Assistant / Laboratory Instructor

UC Riverside — Physics and Astronomy, September 2007 – December 2008
and March 2010 – June 2010. Calculus-based classical mechanics lab,
algebra-based and calculus-based electricity and magnetism lab, and
calculus-based modern physics discussion.

### Undergraduate Researcher

California State University, Fullerton — Physics and Astronomy,
August 2005 – August 2007. High-precision differential cross-section
measurements for electron impact on various targets; designed and
machined a rotary table for variable-geometry scattering experiments.

### Physics Tutor

CSUF — Physics and Astronomy, September 2005 – June 2007. Volunteer
tutor in the department's informal tutoring lounge.
