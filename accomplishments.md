## Science and sonification

<details markdown="1">
<summary><h3 id="black-hole-sonification-accomplishment">Black-hole sonification (2021–present)</h3></summary>

Translated X-ray echo measurements of black holes into audible sound
with astrophysicist Erin Kara (MIT). The sonification was released
alongside the *Astrophysical Journal* echo-mapping research and was
reported worldwide.

**Evidence:**

- [Hear the Weird Sounds of a Black Hole Singing — The New York Times, 7 May 2022](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/05/07/science/space/astronomy-black-hole-sound.html)
- [CNN, 6 May 2022](https://www.cnn.com/2022/05/06/world/black-hole-echo-sounds-scn/index.html)
- [The full coverage record on the CV](/cv.html#black-hole-sonification)

</details>
