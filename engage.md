## Engagement standards

Running a good engagement at this level means a few things up front: a
venue that works for the speakers it has booked, materials shared in
advance in formats that screen readers handle properly, briefings that
describe slides and visuals as a matter of course, and a single
technical contact who can sign off on the production setup with me ahead
of time. For artistic and installation work that contact is
non-negotiable; I will not turn up to commission an installation I have
not been able to verify.

I read with a screen reader. I do not need an accessibility plan layered
on top of the rest of the production — I need the production to be set
up properly the first time. The events that have gone best are the ones
where the host treated this as part of running a competent event at this
level, rather than as a problem to solve in the last week.

The framing this comes from is on the [Philosophy](/philosophy.html)
page. The short version: engagement that produces participation
restrictions is engagement that has been set up badly, not engagement
involving someone who needs less of it.

## Guest lectures and master classes

I am available for graduate seminars, undergraduate guest sessions,
intensive master classes for design and engineering programmes, and
departmental colloquia. Format options include single 90-minute
sessions, half-day workshops, multi-day intensives, and short
residencies. I have run guest sessions at the University of Marburg,
the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, MIT (Comparative Media Studies),
Boston University, and MassArt; master classes and intensives at
Wolfram Summer School, EPFL, Imperial College London, and Hermes
Summer School. Subjects I'm strong on: computational thinking and
inclusive curriculum design; sonification and tactile data
representation; multisensory HCI; humanistic co-design.

## Consulting and advisory

Scope: accessibility audits and remediation strategy; AI-and-disability
strategy; multisensory and sonification product design; integration work
of the kind I delivered into Wolfram\|Alpha and the first computational
answers in Apple Siri; Section 508 / VPAT authoring; standards-body
advice; expert witness and external review. I work with technology
companies, universities, museums, broadcasters, and standards bodies. I
am available for short engagements (a single audit, a strategy memo) or
for retainer-style ongoing advisory. Past clients and collaborators
include Wolfram Research, Apple, the DIAGRAM Center / Benetech, the
W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, TeachAccess, and PhET Interactive
Simulations.

## Research collaboration and PhD supervision

I am actively taking research collaborators and PhD students at the

echolocation training, perception-aware AI systems, and inclusive
human–computer interaction more broadly. I co-publish with my
collaborators and supervise to completion. I welcome enquiries from
