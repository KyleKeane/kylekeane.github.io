## Access Bristol — assistive technology accelerator

*8–9 November 2025, Bill Brown Design Suite, University of Bristol.
Lead industry sponsors: GitHub, University of Bristol,
[Smartbox](https://thinksmartbox.com/).*

A two-day assistive-technology accelerator I organised at Bristol.
Day 1: introductions, meet-and-greets, and team formation. Day 2: team
brainstorming and proposals that could be pursued and lead to funding
and curation. The mission was to learn about the systemic
participation restrictions faced by local community members living
with various disabilities and to work together to identify when, how,
and for whom technology can assist with self-determination and
participation.

[Access Bristol homepage](https://www.accessbristol.org/) ·
[Access Bristol outcomes and highlights reel](https://accessbristol.org/outcomes.html).

## Emergent Harmonics — innovation in assistive tech, art, and echolocation

*14 July 2025, The Watershed, 1 Canon Road, Harbourside, Bristol
(also streamed online). Hosted by Kyle Keane, University of Bristol.*

A one-day symposium on innovation at the intersection of assistive
technology, art, and human echolocation. Daniel Kish (President,
World Access for the Blind) opened with a keynote on Self
Determination. Talks followed by David Johnson (Royal College of Art)
on the aesthetics of the mind, and Dr. Florian Grond (Concordia
University) on sonic boundary objects. The Blind Navigation panel
brought together Daniel Kish, Juan Ruiz, Derrick Twene, and Michelle
Young. Featured student researchers: Kailey Epstein, Abena
Kyereme-Tuah, Dixant Pant, Juan Pablo Ruiz Partida, Dev Raju, Tanish
Raorane, and Alex Thompson.

[Emergent Harmonics on Sound Without Sight](https://soundwithoutsight.org/news/event-bristol-online-emergent-harmonics-innovation-in-assistive-tech-art-echolocation/).
