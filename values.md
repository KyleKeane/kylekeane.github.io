## Overview

Everything on this site serves one value: the
[full participation](/full-participation.html) of disabled people in
STEM and in culture — stated in full, on my terms, on that page. The
value lives through three principles. Each principle below carries a
short statement and a link to its evidence: every tagged
accomplishment in the record that instantiates it.

## Perception beyond sight

The body's non-visual senses are full-resolution channels for complex
information. Science, data, and art that only reach the eyes are
incomplete — the same information can arrive as sound, touch, and
structure, at no loss of rigor. This principle runs through the
[Research](/research.html) and [Creative](/creative.html) personas:
sonification of real astrophysics, audio-tactile graphics,
echolocation training, and installations built from data.

[Every accomplishment evidencing Perception beyond sight](/cv-every-sense.html).

## Build with, not for

Technology worth making is made with the people who will use it.
Community members with disabilities belong inside the design team as
co-designers and experts, not outside it as recipients. This principle
runs through the [Teaching](/teaching.html) persona: the MIT
assistive-technology capstone, the Humanistic Co-Design Initiative's
fellowships across India, Saudi Arabia, and Uruguay, and the hackathons
and accelerators.

[Every accomplishment evidencing Build with, not for](/cv-build-with-people.html).

## One standard of rigor

The work is multidisciplinary by intention, not by accident — the same
training and the same standard apply across quantum physics, software
engineering, education, and art; and when the tools are missing, you
build them. This principle runs through the whole career arc, from the
[Research](/research.html) persona's quantum-information theory to the
Wolfram\|Alpha engineering to the sonification.

[Every accomplishment evidencing One standard of rigor](/cv-rigor.html).

## The value itself

Full participation is both the value above the principles and a
measurable thing in its own right — the
[participation model of disability, my language, and what I expect
from the people who work with me](/full-participation.html) spell it
out, and
[every accomplishment evidencing Full participation](/cv-full-participation.html)
is collected in the archive. For the human-first version of all of
this, start at [About](/) and pick a persona.
