## Grants awarded as PI / co-PI *(from CV)*

### 2022

- **Niche Advice Sharing Social Network for Individuals with
  Disabilities** — Johns Hopkins University, Disability Health Research
  Center.
- **Intercultural Education Exchange** — Al-Faisal University. Research
  visits and workshops, plus funding for a 10-person cohort of mentors
  from MIT to support an accessibility track as part of a 10-day
  game-a-thon.
- **Intercultural Education Exchange** — United States Embassy of
  Uruguay. Funding to help train faculty in facilitating collaborative
  design workshops and co-designing assistive technology with local
  community members.
- **MIT–Brazil Discretionary Seed Fund.** Funding for a student to join
  a humanitarian engineering trip to Uruguay.
- **MIT Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP).** Funded
  multiple undergraduate research projects.

### 2021

- **TranscribeGlass: An affordable heads-up augmented-reality real-time
  captioning device for the Deaf** — India–United States Technology
  Transfer Fund. Funding in partnership with a collaborator in India for
  a very low-cost transcription device for deaf individuals.
- **MIT UROP.**

### 2020

- **CoCreATe Riyadh** — Al-Faisal University. Funding from various Saudi
  sources centrally managed through Al-Faisal University to bring a
  cohort of MIT students for a fellowship launch program and for project
  management and remote mentorship of 20 engineering teams working with
  local individuals living with disabilities.
- **Online Primer for Co-Designing Assistive Technologies** — MITx
  Educational Innovation Fund.
- **Low-cost Vision Support Aids** — MIT Alumni Class Funds. Funding to
  explore manufacturing low-cost vision aids for blind individuals in
  India.
- **TactionTablet: An Affordable Tactile Graphics Display for the
  Blind** — MIT Jameel World Education Laboratory. Funding for a
  year-long MEng student thesis to create an affordable tactile graphics
  display using a novel technique of a moving actuator and passive
  locking tactile pins.
- **MIT UROP.**

### 2019

- **Assistive Technology in the Developing World** — MIT Alumni Class
  Funds. Funding to bring undergraduate and graduate students to India
  for a month-long for-credit course about doing service engineering
  for locals impacted by disabilities.
- **Effectiveness of Infusing Computer Programming into Online
  Introductory Physics Classes** — MIT Integrated Learning Initiative
  (MITili). Funding to deploy a 2×2 factorial design of interventions
  in the online physics curriculum: coding, interactive visualizations,
  standard math derivations, and conceptual reflection.
- **MIT UROP.**
