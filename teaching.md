I teach across programming, electronics prototyping, computational
thinking, multisensory and human-centered design, and the design of
inclusive technology. I supervise PhD, MSc, and undergraduate
research, and I run hands-on workshops, master classes, and short
intensives at universities and design schools. Booking and
collaboration enquiries via the [Engage](/engage.html) page.

## Current teaching at Bristol

### COMS30054 — Interactive Devices

*University of Bristol, School of Computer Science. Co-teacher.*

Design and function of interactive hardware systems, with emphasis on
user interaction, embedded technologies, and the principles behind
modern device interfaces.

### Bristol student supervision

*University of Bristol, School of Computer Science.*

I supervise final-year computer science dissertations and MSc
projects, including a conversion MSc student in Computer Science and a
team of immersive-technology MSc students. Student names, project
titles, and years are added incrementally as projects complete.

## Previously taught at MIT

A condensed list of subjects I led or co-taught at MIT between 2015
and 2024. The full course descriptions and the broader teaching
history are on the [CV](/cv.html) page.

  faculty 2022–2024; teaching team 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019. The

  exchange programme.

  Engineering** (MSE). Lead 2015–2018.
- **3.017 — Modeling, Problem-Solving, Computing, and Visualization**
  (MSE). Lead 2017, 2018.

- **6.a01 — Computing Your Freshman Year** (EECS). Freshman seminar
  on using computer programming to augment first-year physics,
  mathematics, chemistry, biology, and humanities. Fall 2018, 2019.
- **3.a01 — Making in Materials Science and Engineering** (MSE).
  Freshman seminar on rapid prototyping. Fall 2016.
- **ES.S09 — Disability and the Criminal Justice System** (MIT

## International workshops and intensives

### Humanistic Co-Design Initiative — Fellowship Launch Weekends

A three-day community engagement workshop educating engineering and
design students about disability, accessibility, and assistive
technology before facilitating team formation with a local community
partner with a disability, around a year-long co-design project.

- UTEC, Uruguay — June 2022.
- Al-Faisal University, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — May 2022 and
  January 2019.
- IIT Delhi, India — January 2018, January 2019.
- IIT Madras, Chennai, India — January 2018, January 2019.
- LV Prasad Eye Institute, Hyderabad, India — January 2018.
- Independent education startup, Chennai, India — July 2017.

### Intentionally Inclusive Interaction Design

Ten-day accessibility course on the fundamentals of human–computer
interaction and the landscape of assistive technology; students
complete an independent project demonstrating a functional interface
compatible with a specific assistive technology. Sichuan University,
China and Al-Faisal University, Saudi Arabia — June 2022 / May 2022.

### Applied Interdisciplinary Artificial Intelligence

Three-day hands-on workshop introducing machine learning and AI as a
tool for researchers outside computer science. Sichuan University and
Al-Faisal University — June 2022 / May 2022.

### Design of Assistive Technology — BeaverWorks Summer Institute

Four-week intensive summer programme for high-school juniors and
seniors, pairing students with local community members with
disabilities for a co-design project. MIT Lincoln Labs BeaverWorks
Summer Institute — Summer 2018, 2019, 2020.

### Learn CAD and 3D Printing by Making Accessible Teaching Aids

Semester-long weekend programme for middle-school students learning
computer-aided design by modifying existing tactile puzzle templates
into custom teaching aids for local blind students. MIT Lincoln Labs
BeaverWorks Summer Institute — Spring 2021.
